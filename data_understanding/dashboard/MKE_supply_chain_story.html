The three traps that make "the data is there" a lie
SEMI-ADDITIVITY

Summing on-hand across a week gives 538,116 — the truth is 83,254. 6.5× wrong, no error thrown.

MAGNITUDE ILLUSION

The "biggest 17.6M-unit crisis" was a $0.0134 carbide part at a plant — ~$235K, un-expeditable. Rank by $×ABC, not units.

TIME-BLINDNESS

Week Oct-5 read healthy (PAB 151,929) in June; by August the same week was a breach (PAB −3,464).

The write-boundary guardrail (non-negotiable)

prod_edw / prod_operations are READ-ONLY. Every derived table lands in sandbox_operations.po_management; transactional app state lives in Lakebase; external writeback (Oracle Fusion / SCC) goes through a typed, approved, audited action path — never a direct prod write. This is architectural, so no production record can be harmed by the app.

Run shape · cadence · recovery
CADENCE

Weekly Monday Lakeflow Job (profiling → validate → SDP → Gold → metric refresh → publish → notify). Snapshot history retained ⇒ "was green, now red" is queryable.

IDEMPOTENT

Re-running a week creates no duplicates (T2). Backfill 180 days; late-data handling; row-count parity check after each Silver build.

GOVERNED

Deployed via DABs + CI/CD (dev→prod), source-controlled — not notebook-only. UC lineage end-to-end; all writes to sandbox only.
